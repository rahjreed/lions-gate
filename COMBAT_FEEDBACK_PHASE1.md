# Combat feedback — Phase 1

- `MAX_LIVES = 5` is shared by new runs, retries, save validation, hearts and Judah's health bar. Each unprotected incorrect answer or timeout loses exactly one life. Shield protections and cosmetic armor are unchanged.
- Existing carry-over rules remain: clearing a level carries remaining lives into the next level; it does not heal Judah. New runs and retries restore five. Existing version-3 saves retain their exact remaining lives, including zero-life defeats, scores, queues, rewards, equipment, achievements and checkpoints. No new storage key or forced migration is introduced.
- Enemy health still derives from correct answers in the current ten-question level. A normal hit removes ten health points. Imperfect successful clears may leave enemy health above zero, as before.
- At the existing 240ms impact callback, transient numbers derive from the actual before/after health delta: `-10` beside the opponent, `-1 LIFE` beside Judah, or `BLOCKED` when a shield prevents loss. The renderer never applies damage. Labels flash, rise and fade for 620ms, stay inside the arena on mobile, pause with existing effects and clean up on navigation. Reduced motion keeps a static readable label.
- Original oscillator sound patches provide a short sword swoosh at the existing 140ms strike phase, a layered contact hit, a lower damage thud, a metallic shield block and an extra heavy impact on Gatekeeper hits or a finishing blow. Combat patches are under 180ms, with conservative gain headroom. Existing success/reward sounds, independent SFX/Music controls, unlock/resume fallback and mute cancellation remain.
- No question content, selection, scoring, answer validation, combat timing, sprites, armor behavior or power inventory rules were changed. No recorded or copyrighted sound assets were added.

Verification uses `node scripts/build-site.mjs`, including the dedicated `test-combat-feedback.mjs` plus updated life/health/retry tests. The optional legacy Playwright regression now skips the pre-battle introduction before answering. Actual browser checks are performed through the supported browser tools on isolated loopback saves; the user's normal save is untouched. Physical iOS/Android speaker output and perceived loudness require device review.

Local results: production build and all 21 configured suites passed. Additional content, curriculum and campaign checks passed. Browser inspection captured actual `-10`, `-1 LIFE` and `BLOCKED` frames at 390px/320px widths, verified enemy health 100→90, lives 5→4, shield retention at 4, and timeout loss 5→4. With SFX off, a further hit reduced enemy health 90→80 while the audio play counter stayed unchanged. The audio context ran successfully with SFX on. Existing combat-boundary pause/cancellation tests still pass; no physical-device listening or installed-PWA relaunch is claimed.
