# Biblical curriculum and doctrine

Permanent editorial policy for **Kingdom Race: The Lion's Gate** in the existing `lions-gate-sword-sharpener` repository. This document establishes curriculum requirements, not a game rename or runtime migration. The project owner has final editorial authority. No policy statement itself approves a question.

## Authority, sources and interpretive discipline

Scripture is the primary source of religious instruction. The curriculum is Israelite-centered: Yah's authority, commandments, Torah, the twelve tribes, genealogies, covenants, promises, inheritance, restoration and reunification of Israel, and the enduring authority of Yah's Word. Teach Yeshua and the Apostles in their scriptural settings, with their connections to the Torah and Prophets. Include prophecy about the Messiah, Israel, the nations and the Kingdom; righteous leadership, government, judgment and accountability.

Do not assume a commandment is obsolete because it originates in the Torah. Examine its actual wording, recipients, conditions, exceptions and scriptural connections. Do not substitute mainstream denominational assumptions, personal research documents or unverified commentary for the text. Distinguish an explicit statement, a stated purpose, an inference and a present-day application. When reasonable readers dispute an interpretation, test an explicit textual observation or clearly name the interpretive framework being evaluated and cite its supporting passages. An Israelite-centered curriculum does not turn an unstated inference into an uncontested fact.

Use biblical names, tribes, nations, genealogies and covenant terminology. Do not invent modern racial classifications or identify modern populations with biblical peoples. Do not mock denominations or their adherents. Religious identity does not excuse unfair, ambiguous or unsupported answer keys.

Preserve the established **traditional 80-book KJV collection**: 39 Old Testament, 27 New Testament and the traditional 14 KJV Apocrypha books. KJV is the required default under current validation. Clearly identify every source book, including Apocrypha supporting study and any already-reviewed questions using those books. This policy neither removes existing Apocrypha eligibility nor expands the canon. Baruch includes the Letter of Jeremiah; the Song of the Three Holy Children includes the Prayer of Azariah under the existing collection convention. Enoch, Jubilees, Jasher and additional Cepher writings remain excluded. Short stories, Myles Awakens, Becoming Solomon and Yahweh Strickster remain excluded. Personal notes may inspire topics; players must never need them to answer a question.

Never fabricate quotations, references, source checks or approval evidence. Read cited passages in context and distinguish a KJV quotation from an explanatory paraphrase. Automated validation is not independent Scripture verification. A pending excerpt marker is an unfinished task, not passage evidence ready for approval.

## Scope and systematic coverage

The long-term objective is every identifiable commandment, ordinance and judgment throughout Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers and Deuteronomy, with relevant Prophets, Yeshua and apostolic connections. Do not assert a fixed exhaustive commandment count before establishing the cataloging method. The tracker starts with **187 Torah chapter slots**, not 187 completed chapter audits. Existing related questions show partial coverage, not complete treatment of a chapter or commandment.

| Topic | Required coverage and progression |
| --- | --- |
| 01 Covenants and commandments | Parties, promises, obligations, signs, stated purposes and relations between passages. |
| 02 Sabbath and rest | Stated day, recipients, rest, purposes, boundaries, mercy and contextual applications. |
| 03 Appointed times | All feasts and holy convocations, their instructions and supported connections. |
| 04 Dietary distinctions | Clean/unclean categories, criteria, blood, carcasses, actors and conditions. |
| 05 Appearance and modesty | Fringes, beards, hair, clothing and modesty; explicit requirements versus added grooming rules. |
| 06 Prayer, worship and holiness | Worship instructions, prayer, covenant settings and holiness requirements. |
| 07 Marriage, family and lineage | Covenant obligations, prohibited relationships, household duties and inheritance contexts. |
| 08 Vulnerable people and household duties | Parents, children, widows, orphans and strangers; protections and responsibilities. |
| 09 Property and inheritance | Theft, boundaries, restitution, loss, ownership and inheritance conditions. |
| 10 Economic justice | Lending, interest, debt, wages, tithes, pledges and fair treatment. |
| 11 Land and agricultural cycles | Agriculture, gleaning, Sabbatical years and Jubilee; timing, beneficiaries and exceptions. |
| 12 Priesthood and sanctuary | Roles, sanctuary regulations, offerings, sacrifices and conditional priestly applications. |
| 13 Judicial procedure | Witnesses, evidence, judges, jurisdiction, judgments and procedural safeguards. |
| 14 Sexual conduct and purity | Prohibited conduct, purity conditions, distinctions and restoration procedures. |
| 15 Idolatry and prohibited worship | Named practices, warnings and the distinction between commandments and traditions. |

| 17 National restoration and land | Tribes, covenants, reunification, promised land and inheritance. |
| 18 Prophetic judgment and Kingdom government | Messiah's reign, nations, justice, repentance, mercy, punishment and responsibility. |

Historical people, places, events and other foundational scriptural study remain part of the curriculum. The tracker uses an additional general-foundations index rather than forcing every such item into a Torah-law topic.

For each identified law or teaching, record the exact passage, speaker, addressee, setting, command/prohibition/promise, stated reason, conditions, exceptions and related passages. Preserve distinctions between priestly, household, judicial and national responsibilities. Link separate learning objectives to stable question IDs; one question need not cover all those dimensions.

## Appointed times

Maintain individual coverage plans for **Passover, Unleavened Bread, Firstfruits, Weeks/Pentecost, Memorial of Trumpets, Day of Atonement, Tabernacles and the Eighth Day**, plus **weekly Sabbath** and **relevant new-moon passages**. Do not collapse adjacent observances into an interchangeable festival or turn a passage's silence into an invented rule.

Each of these ten tracker plans has six required study dimensions:

1. Locate the stated date, day, season or counting instruction; distinguish explicit wording from a calendar calculation.
2. Identify the stated duration, boundaries and any separate assembly or rest day.
3. Identify commanded observances, work/rest instructions, offerings where applicable, participants and conditions.
4. Identify purposes or memorial explanations actually stated in the text.
5. Explain historical and covenant context and distinguish later narrative practice from an express command.
6. Investigate supported prophetic, Gospel and apostolic connections; name an interpretive framework when the proposed connection is disputed.

These are coverage objectives, not claims that the date or every detail is identical across the observances. For new-moon passages in particular, inventory what each passage actually says rather than assuming uniform instructions. The initial source sweep should include Torah feast instructions and their narrative/prophetic/apostolic connections; exact ranges must be recorded and verified during cataloging. Do not introduce speculative calendar disputes unless the owner explicitly authorizes that study.

## Marriage, family, lineage and inheritance

Study relevant passages in Genesis, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Ezra, Nehemiah, the Gospels and apostolic writings. Track covenant obligations, prohibited relationships, parents and children, marriage/divorce/remarriage settings, lineage and inheritance. Record the named peoples, recipients and conditions rather than inventing a universal rule from a special case. Neither dismiss ancient commandments automatically nor insert modern racial identifications. Separate direct wording from interpretive conclusions and avoid silently broadening a restriction beyond its stated scope.

## Prophecy and Kingdom government

Cover difficult and severe passages as well as comforting ones: Israel's restoration and land inheritance; the Messiah's reign; the continued existence and accountability of nations; nations learning Yah's laws; judgment upon rebellious nations; the rod of iron in Psalm 2 and Revelation; Tabernacles in Zechariah 14; righteous judgment and governmental responsibility; mercy, repentance, justice and punishment.

For each passage, distinguish what it depicts from a proposed timetable or harmonization. Explain the speaker, agent, recipients and conditions of judgment. **Divine prophetic judgment is not permission for present-day people to enact violence.** Do not omit severe judgments merely to make the material comfortable, and do not convert descriptive prophecy into an instruction for players to punish others.

## Educational depth and difficulty: editorial scale 1–10

Difficulty measures reasoning, not obscure names, vocabulary, unfamiliar practices or agreement with an interpretation. Basic Sabbath, fringes and grooming questions are not automatically master-level. Sophisticated conditions, exceptions and priestly/judicial applications can be advanced subjects.

| Rating | Reasoning requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Recognize a clear person, event, command or explicit statement familiar to ordinary Bible readers. |
| 2 | Explain a straightforward commandment or a reason directly stated in one passage. |
| 3 | Read a passage's immediate context, sequence, recipient or simple qualification. |
| 4 | Connect related verses or identify a quotation and its source. |
| 5 | Compare contexts, stated purposes or covenant settings without flattening their distinctions. |
| 6 | Integrate several passages and distinguish a supported conclusion from an unsupported claim. |
| 7 | Apply multiple stated conditions to a bounded law, judgment or leadership scenario. |
| 8 | Resolve interacting conditions/exceptions across passages with different actors or responsibilities. |
| 9 | Evaluate difficult prophetic or legal connections and competing explanations using explicit textual constraints. |
| 10 | Sustain substantial multi-book reasoning or complex application, explain the evidence chain and limits, and distinguish inference from text. |

Master-level material must require substantial cross-reference, context or application. Merely lengthening a stem or replacing common names does not increase its educational depth. Pilot difficulty with readers and review performance without changing a published question's identity silently.

**Compatibility boundary:** the editorial rubric remains 1–10; runtime display remains 1–3 and complexity 1–7. The tested, opt-in adapter in [LOCAL_QUESTION_REVIEW.md](LOCAL_QUESTION_REVIEW.md) maps newly explicit calibrations only. Supplied KR source ratings and all current runtime values are preserved unless a reviewer deliberately enters a calibration. That decision and its mapping version are exported in fingerprinted knowledge metadata. Earlier topics continue across the seven overlapping stages. No candidate has been remapped or published; selector behavior, including small-pool fallback, is unchanged. Human calibration and adequate approved coverage remain necessary before expecting sustained advanced/master content.

## Question and review standards

Questions should develop commandment understanding, stated reasons, book-to-book connections, New Testament quotations of earlier Scripture, context, bounded application, unsupported-claim recognition, complex law/judgment scenarios and prophetic understanding. Preserve accessible beginnings while building toward deeper study.

Each candidate needs four plausible alternatives and one defensible intended answer; precise primary and supporting references; an educational explanation; category, concept and difficulty metadata; and a review status. Avoid absurd distractors, answer-length clues, needless answer repetition in stems and unsupported theological claims. Check reference/quotation accuracy, surrounding context and why all three distractors are wrong for the exact question. A named interpretive framework does not remove the need for evidence.

Purposeful reinforcement across different passages is welcome. Review exact duplicates and answer disclosure separately from subject overlap. Existing selection hard-blocks certain exact answer-phrase conflicts, but shared concepts receive only a soft penalty and short/paraphrased disclosures can escape detection. Do not promise separation without checking the actual selector. See QUESTION_PUBLISHING_AUDIT.md.

All new records remain unpublished candidates until manual review and the owner's explicit publication authorization. The local review app records explicit decisions and evidence; it is a trusted local-operator tool, not proof of independent review. Publication requires current ledger approval and checked passage evidence. Never mark a check true merely because a validator passed. Correct a draft before approving it; changing fingerprinted content after approval requires renewed review. Preserve published IDs and saved queues.

## Maintenance and document relationship

- This document governs curriculum/editorial intent; BIBLICAL_KNOWLEDGE_ENGINE.md describes runtime mechanics and compatibility. Do not read the 1–10 policy as a hidden gameplay change.
- `content/curriculum-coverage.json` inventories current records and planned Torah chapters/appointed-time objectives. Its automatic topic associations are provisional. Candidate coverage is never counted as live reviewed coverage.
- `scripts/build-curriculum-coverage.mjs` refreshes that derived inventory without modifying questions. Future manually cataloged commands should be stored separately and merged explicitly; do not bury manual research in generated fields that a refresh overwrites.
- `QUESTION_PUBLISHING_AUDIT.md` records current safeguards, commands and remaining human review responsibilities. Neither these documents nor a Git push performs approval or publication.
- Before a later question batch, consult the tracker for gaps, verify the proposed source passages, calibrate difficulty and preserve the owner's review authority. This task creates no questions and approves none.
