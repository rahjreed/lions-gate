## Progression and acceptance gates

The current selector reaches target complexity 7 at Level 525 and saturates afterward. Gatekeepers request a higher target up to that cap; the 7:3 focus/general mix and trap priorities also constrain selection. A plentiful overall bank cannot compensate for missing advanced general/trap items. Where a passage genuinely fits, spread reviewed context/tradition/misquote objectives and general history/prophetic connections across the higher bands; do not falsely tag every difficult item as a trap or relabel its topic just to satisfy quotas.

Align authoring to the proposed final campaign: 451–525 integrated discernment; 526–600 righteous judgment; 601–650 leadership under pressure; 651–699 cumulative reasoning; Level 700 culminating Gatekeeper. The plan does not change runtime selection or combat. Increasing challenge after 525 requires a separate reported proposal, balanced against saves, timers, lives and existing content.

Before claiming an advanced release or the Level 700 campaign is ready, require: approved source-bound records; adequate distinct concepts and passages across strands; held-out learner calibration; ten-question non-conflicting sets at every stage and Gatekeeper; measured category/trap availability, target-vs-selected complexity, repeated exposure and fresh-retry behavior; preserved saved queues; and no unreviewed fallback. Repetition is permitted for reinforcement and should be measured, not concealed. Counts alone cannot establish educational or gameplay readiness.
