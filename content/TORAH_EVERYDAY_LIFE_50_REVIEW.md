### torah-judgment-majority-pressure

- Category: Civil laws, judges, and righteous judgment; curriculum 13: Judges, witnesses and procedure.
- Editorial difficulty: 3/10. Objective: Distinguish truthful testimony from pressure to support a majority.
- **Question:** A witness faces pressure to support the larger group in a lawsuit. Which course accords with Exodus 23:1–2?
  - A. Give truthful testimony rather than bend judgment toward the crowd. **(proposed correct)**
  - B. Support the larger group when its members have higher standing.
  - C. Let the larger group settle what the witness should testify.
  - D. Repeat the group’s report if the witness did not start it.
- Scripture: [Exodus 23:1-2](https://www.kingjamesbibleonline.org/Exodus-Chapter-23/).
- Explanation: The commands prohibit raising a false report, joining an unrighteous witness, and following a multitude to distort judgment. Group size does not make testimony true.
