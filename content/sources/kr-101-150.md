# SECTION 1: THE LAW AND ITS ENDURING AUTHORITY

### KR-101 | Difficulty 2
**Question:** According to Deuteronomy 4:2, what restriction did Yah place upon Israel concerning His commandments?

A. Israel could modify commandments when necessary.  
B. Israel could replace commandments with traditions.

D. Only priests were required to follow the commandments.

**Correct: C**

**Scripture:** Deuteronomy 4:2

**Explanation:** Yah instructed Israel not to add to or subtract from His commandments. His instructions were not to be altered according to human preference.
